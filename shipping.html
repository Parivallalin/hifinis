Hifinis — Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Curvio Studio · parivallalin@gmail.com

Hifinis and the Hifinis unlock are digital products. We do not sell or ship any physical goods, so there are no shipping charges and no delivery address is needed.

How the unlock is delivered

  • Google Play: the app is unlocked as soon as Google Play confirms the payment, normally within seconds.
  • Our own checkout (Razorpay): after you pay, return to Hifinis. The app collects your licence key by itself and unlocks, normally within a minute. The confirmation page shows your order number; keep it, with your payment receipt, for restoring later.

If the app has not unlocked

  1. Make sure the phone is online and open Hifinis again.
  2. Google Play purchase: open Settings in Hifinis and tap Restore.
  3. Checkout purchase: tap Restore and enter your order number (it starts with HF-) and the email address or phone number you paid with.

If it is still locked 10 minutes after paying, email parivallalin@gmail.com with your order number. We reply within 2 working days and will either deliver the key or refund the payment, as set out in the refund policy.

New phone

A Google Play unlock follows your Google account. A checkout purchase can be restored on up to three of your own devices with the Restore steps above.
