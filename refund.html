Hifinis — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Curvio Studio · parivallalin@gmail.com

You can use every feature of Hifinis for 14 days before paying anything, so please try it on your own phone and audio gear first. If you buy the unlock and it is not right for you, this page explains how to get your money back.

Purchases through our own checkout (Razorpay)

  • Within 7 days of payment: we refund the full amount on request, for any reason.
  • After 7 days: we refund if Hifinis stops working on the device you bought it for and we cannot fix the problem within a reasonable time.
  • Charged twice, or charged without getting a key: we refund the extra or failed payment in full at any time.

To ask for a refund, email parivallalin@gmail.com from the address you paid with, and include your order number (it starts with HF- and is on your payment receipt). We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the payment method you used (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it; your bank or payment provider usually credits it within 5 to 7 working days after that. When a purchase is refunded, its licence key is cancelled.

Purchases on Google Play

Google Play handles these payments, so the refund comes from Google:

  • Within 48 hours: ask Google directly from your order history at play.google.com or through Google Play's refund page.
  • After 48 hours and within 7 days of payment: email us your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we will refund it through Google Play.

Google returns the money to the payment method on your Google account, on Google's own timeline.

If money left your account but the payment failed

Sometimes a bank debits an amount for a payment that did not complete. Banks reverse these automatically, usually within 5 to 7 working days. If the money has not come back by then, email us the date, amount and any reference number and we will follow it up with the payment provider.

Cancellation

The unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is nothing to cancel and you are never charged again. If you start a checkout and change your mind, simply close the page; no money is taken until you approve the payment. Once a payment is complete, the refund rules above apply.

Promo codes and free keys

Unlocks obtained with a promo code or a free key were not paid for and cannot be exchanged for money.
