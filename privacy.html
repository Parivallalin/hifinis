Hifinis — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Curvio Studio · parivallalin@gmail.com

  • No adsand no tracking
  • No accountnothing to sign up for
  • Your music stayson your phone
  • Only a purchase recordreaches Curvio Studio

Hifinis is an offline audiophile music player. Your music, your listening habits, and your files stay on your device. Hifinis has no accounts and no advertising, and Curvio Studio does not track you. The only information Curvio Studio receives is the purchase record described under Purchases below. Two Google components inside the app send Google limited diagnostics — see Diagnostics sent by Google components below.

Data we do not collect

Hifinis does not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share: your music files, library contents, playlists, favorites, play history, settings, location or contacts. Apart from the purchase record described under Purchases, nothing about you is transmitted to Curvio Studio.

Music library access

You choose the folders Hifinis may read using Android's system folder picker. Hifinis indexes the audio files in those folders (titles, artists, albums, artwork) into a local database on your device only. Removing a folder or uninstalling the app removes that index.

Artist images (Deezer)

When "Artist images" is enabled (Settings → Library & appearance), Hifinis sends the artist name being displayed to the Deezer API (api.deezer.com) to fetch a public artist photo. The artist name is the only data sent; results are cached on your device. Deezer's own privacy policy applies to that request. You can turn this off at any time.

Artist pages (Wikipedia, Wikidata)

With "Artist images" on, opening an artist's page also sends that artist's name to Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org) and Wikidata (wikidata.org), both run by the Wikimedia Foundation, to show a public photo, a short biography and facts such as birth date and genres. The artist name is the only data sent; the result is stored on your device so each artist is looked up once. The Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy applies to those requests. Biography text is shown under its CC BY-SA licence with a link to the article.

Purchases (Google Play version)

The Google Play version can be tried in full for 14 days; after that a one-time in-app purchase unlocks it. Purchases and promo codes are handled entirely by Google Play: Hifinis never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the unlock, and keeps a record of the answer on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Once the app is unlocked, it sends our licence server a purchase record so we can count unlocks and help you if a purchase goes missing: a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256) of Google Play's purchase token, the Google Play order number if there is one, whether the unlock was bought or redeemed with a promo code, the app version, and a device code. The device code is a scrambled value made from your phone's Android ID and the app; it cannot be turned back into the Android ID and does not name you. You may also choose to add an email address to the record so we can find your purchase when you write to us; this is optional and the app works the same without it.

Purchases (our own checkout)

On phones without Google Play, the unlock can be bought through our own checkout. The payment is processed by Razorpay; we never see or store your card, bank or UPI details, and Razorpay's privacy policy applies to the payment. Our licence server keeps the order: its number, amount and status, the device code described above, the Razorpay payment reference, and the email address and phone number you gave at checkout (the phone number is kept as its last ten digits). We use these only to issue your licence key, to check that it is you when you restore the purchase on another device, to handle refunds, and to keep the records the law requires. If you enter a licence key with an email address, that address is stored with the key.

Where purchase records are kept, and deleting them

Purchase records are stored on Cloudflare (our hosting provider) and are not sold, shared for advertising, or combined with anything else. We keep them for as long as the licence is in use and as long as tax and accounting law requires. To see, correct or delete the record of your purchase, email parivallalin@gmail.com; we will delete everything we are not legally required to keep. Deleting an order means it can no longer be restored on a new device.

Online lyrics (LRCLIB)

When "Fetch lyrics online" is enabled (Settings → Lock screen & widgets) and a song has no synced lyrics of its own, Hifinis sends that song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), an open lyrics database, to look up its lyrics. Nothing else is sent: no account, no identifier and no file. The results are cached on your device, so each song is looked up at most once. You can turn this off at any time. With artist images and online lyrics off and no network source added, Hifinis makes no network requests of its own (apart from the update check below, in versions downloaded from our website, and the Google components described under Diagnostics sent by Google components).

Update check (website / GitHub version only)

The version of Hifinis downloaded from our website asks GitHub (api.github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.) about twice a day whether a newer version has been published, and downloads it from GitHub only when you tap Update. These requests carry no personal data — GitHub sees only your IP address and the app version, as with any web request. You can turn the check off in Settings → About → Notify me about updates. The Google Play version has no such check; Play handles its updates.

Microphone permission (visualizer)

Android requires the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) permission for its audio-visualizer API, which Hifinis uses solely to compute the on-screen spectrum of the music Hifinis itself is playing. Hifinis does not record, store, or transmit any audio from the microphone. You may deny this permission; only the visualizer screen is affected.

USB devices

With your permission, Hifinis talks directly to USB DACs you connect, purely to play audio. Device details (model, supported formats) are read locally and never leave your device.

Network music sources (optional)

Hifinis can play music from places other than your phone's storage — a home media server (Jellyfin, Plex, Subsonic/Navidrome), a network share (SMB, WebDAV/Nextcloud, DLNA/UPnP, FTP), a cloud drive (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), a streaming service you already subscribe to (Tidal, Qobuz, SoundCloud), or internet radio. None of this exists until you add a source and enter its address and your own credentials.

  • Those credentials (passwords, OAuth tokens) are encrypted with the Android Keystore and stored only on your device. They are sent only to the server or service you pointed Hifinis at, over its own connection, to log you in and fetch your music.
  • Curvio Studio never receives them and operates no proxy or relay — the connection is between your phone and the server you chose.
  • Each third-party service's own privacy policy and terms apply to what it does with the requests your phone makes to it.
  • Removing a source deletes its stored credentials from your device.

Lyrics translation

Lyric translation runs entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit on-device translation. The first time you pick a language, Android downloads that language model from Google's servers; after that, translation works offline. Your lyrics are never uploaded — the text is translated locally and is not sent anywhere.

Chromecast

Hifinis includes Google's Cast SDK so you can play music on a Chromecast or other Cast device on your network. When you cast, the song is streamed from your phone to the device you picked.

Diagnostics sent by Google components

Two Google components built into Hifinis, ML Kit (lyrics translation) and the Google Cast SDK (Chromecast), send Google limited diagnostic and usage information so Google can keep them working: error codes and performance figures, the languages you translate between, Cast device discovery and session events, your device model, Android version and app version, and a per-installation identifier. This information goes to Google, not to Curvio Studio, and is handled under Google's privacy policy. It never includes your music, your files or your lyrics. Curvio Studio does not receive any of this information.

Editing your files

The tag editor writes the changes you make (title, artist, album, artwork and so on) back into your own audio files, on your device. Nothing about those edits is transmitted.

Backups

The in-app Backup feature writes a file to a location you choose, containing your settings and playlists. It stays wherever you save it; we never receive it.

Children

Hifinis is a general-audience music player and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
