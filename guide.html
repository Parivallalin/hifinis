Hifinis User Guide

For Hifinis 2.5.11 · Updated 2 November 2026 · Curvio Studio

Hifinis is a music player for Android built around exact, untouched sound: it plays your own files on the phone, wired headphones, Bluetooth and USB DACs, with a full set of sound tools, an Audio Studio and a Composer.

1. Getting started

Install Hifinis from Google Play, allow it to read your music, and it finds your songs by itself. Everything is unlocked for 14 days; after that one payment keeps it.

  1. Install Hifinis from Google Play and open it.
  2. Allow access to your music. Hifinis shows Hifinis needs access to your music library — tap Grant access. Also allow notifications, so the player controls appear on the lock screen and in the notification shade.
  3. Let it scan. Your library fills in by itself. Later scans only read what changed, so they are quick.
  4. Pick your folders (optional). Go to Settings → Library → Music folders → Add a music folder to add a folder Android does not index, such as a USB stick or a folder with a .nomedia file.
  5. Choose a language (optional). Hifinis follows your phone's language. To change it: Settings → Language. Thirteen languages are included.

Reading the sound path

  • The pill under the transport shows the file (format, bit depth, sample rate, bitrate), the engine and the output, plus a quality chip such as CD quality, Hi-Res or Lossy. Tap it for the full Signal path: every step from the file to your ears and whether anything changes the sound.

  • SOFT VOL means Hifinis is lowering the volume digitally. Use your DAC's hardware volume to keep the sound bit-perfect (see USB DACs).

5. Audio engines and outputs

Hifinis has three engines; pick one on Home (Android · Hi-Res · USB DAC) or in Settings → Quick settings → Engine (Android · Bit-perfect · USB Exclusive). Hi-Res and Bit-perfect are the same engine.

EngineHow the sound travelsBest forTrade-off
AndroidThrough Android's own player and mixerAny output; casting; the most compatibleAndroid usually converts every song to 48 kHz
Hi-Res (Bit-perfect)Hifinis' own engine, straight to the output. On Android 14+ with a USB DAC it uses Android's bit-perfect mode, so each song plays at its own rateWired headphones, Bluetooth, USB DACs while other apps keep soundOn outputs without a bit-perfect path, Android still converts after Hifinis
USB ExclusiveHifinis talks to the USB DAC directly, without Android in betweenThe purest sound from a USB DAC: every rate the DAC supports, native DSD, hardware volumeOther apps can't use the DAC while Hifinis holds it

Which should I use?

  • Phone speaker or Bluetooth → Hi-Res (or Android if something misbehaves).
  • Wired headphones in the phone's jack → Hi-Res.
  • USB DAC or dongle → USB Exclusive for the best sound; Hi-Res if you also want notifications, calls and other apps to keep using it.
  • USB headsets with a microphone (gaming headsets, earbuds with a mic) start on Hi-Res, so calls and the mic keep working. You can still pick USB Exclusive by hand.

Settings that only make sense for one engine are greyed out or labelled on the others.

Outputs: playing on several devices at once

Tap the Outputs button on Now Playing to see every output: phone speaker, wired, Bluetooth, USB DAC.

  • Tick several to play on all of them together, for example a DAC and a Bluetooth speaker.
  • Play only here sends the music to one output and leaves the others alone.
  • Each output has its own volume, left/right balance and a sync delay to line them up.

Phone Hi-Res (offload)

On some phones (Qualcomm, Android 11 and older) the wired jack can play 44.1 kHz and above only through the phone's audio chip. Turn on Settings → Audio & output → Audio outputs → Phone Hi-Res offload to use it. The phone's chip then decodes the file itself, so Hifinis' EQ and effects don't apply.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth always re-encodes the sound with its own codec (SBC, AAC, aptX, LDAC), so it can never be bit-perfect. The codec is chosen in Android's Developer options, not by apps. Hifinis sends the best signal it can and shows the route in the sound-path pill.

Calls and other apps

When a call rings or another app starts playing, Hifinis pauses and, in USB Exclusive, hands the DAC back so the ringtone can sound. After the call it can resume by itself (Settings → Audio & output → Audio focus → Resume after call).

6. USB DACs

Plug in the DAC, allow Hifinis to use it, choose USB DAC on Home, and every song plays at its own sample rate, untouched.

First connection

  1. Plug the DAC or dongle into the phone's USB-C port.
  2. Android asks whether Hifinis may use the device — tap OK (tick always if offered).
  3. On Home, tap USB DAC (USB Exclusive). To switch automatically every time a DAC is plugged in, turn on Settings → Audio & output → Audio focus → Switch to exclusive USB for a DAC.
  4. Press play. The pill on Now Playing shows USB EXCLUSIVE · BIT-PERFECT and the cover shows ✓ HIFINIS PURE.

Volume: software, hardware, or fixed

Choose in Quick settings → DAC volume or on the DAC's own page (Volume type).

Volume typeHow the level is setBit-perfect?
HardwareInside the DAC, using its own volume controlYes, at every level
SoftwareHifinis lowers the samples digitallyOnly at 100%
AutoHardware when the DAC has a volume control, software otherwiseDepends on the DAC
Fixed at 100% (bit-perfect)Hifinis never changes the level; use the DAC's own buttons or knobYes
  • Each DAC remembers its own volume, separately from the phone's.
  • Ear safety (off by default): starts at a safe level after plugging in and caps sudden jumps. Turn it on under Settings → Audio & output → DAC volume & ear safety.
  • The phone's volume keys and the lock-screen − / + buttons control the DAC volume while Hifinis plays.

Sample rates and DSD

  • Resampling is off by default: every song plays at its own rate. If a DAC can't play a rate, the Resampler page decides what to convert it to.
  • Rates above 96 kHz (on each DAC's page) allows 176.4 kHz, 192 kHz and higher.
  • DSD: played natively when the DAC supports it, or as DoP (DSD over PCM) otherwise.
  • Fade in after a rate change (under USB DAC quirks): a one-second fade when the rate changes between songs, so a DAC that re-locks never starts loud.

Sharing the DAC with other apps

In USB Exclusive, Hifinis holds the DAC; other apps and the ringtone can't use it.

  • Release DAC (Outputs sheet, or the DAC's page) hands it back without unplugging. Press play to take it again.
  • Release while paused gives the DAC back automatically a moment after you pause.
  • Reconnect DAC lets go completely and claims it again — use it if another app grabbed the DAC and Hifinis went quiet.
  • During a call Hifinis releases the DAC by itself and takes it back afterwards.

Your devices

Settings → Your devices lists every DAC, Bluetooth device and headset you've used, each with its own settings (volume type, rates, profile). A device page also shows what it supports: sample rates, bit depth, hardware volume, native DSD, and whether Android offers a bit-perfect mixer for it. USB DAC console shows a live report for troubleshooting.

7. Shaping the sound

The Sound tab holds the equalizer, effects and advanced settings. Anything that changes the sound makes the path no longer bit-perfect, and the ✓ HIFINIS PURE badge goes away while it is on.

Equalizer

  1. Open the Sound tab → Equalizer and switch it on.
  2. Pick a preset: tap a card (Vocal Boost, Rock and so on), a chip (Normal, Classical, Dance…), or Browse all 120. Search finds presets by name. The list includes Hifinis' own full-range curves.
  3. Choose the bands: 5, 10, 15 or 31 bands, each ±15 dB.
  4. Shape it: drag the points on the curve. With Locked on, a tap opens one band's exact value instead, so you can't change bands by accident; Unlocked lets you drag freely.
  5. Save your own: My EQ keeps your curve — hold it to update it. Flat zeroes every band; Reset goes back to the preset.

Parametric EQ gives you exact control: each filter has its own type (bell, shelf, pass and more), frequency, gain and Q. When it is on, it takes over from the graphic EQ.

Tone

The Tone card has four simple sliders — Bass, Vocals, Treble and Loudness — and a switch between Bit-perfect (nothing applied) and My sound (your tone and effects). Loudness follows your volume: it adds bass and treble as you turn down, so quiet listening still sounds full. Use my sound on this DAC remembers the choice per device.

Effects

EffectWhat it does
EnhanceAdds clarity and depth. Choose from 100 voicings
SpatialWidens the stereo image
CrossfeedBlends a little of each channel into the other, so headphones sound less "inside your head"
ReverbAdds room ambience
ReplayGainEvens out loudness between songs (Track) or keeps an album's own dynamics (Album). Peak protection stops clipping

Processor Rack

Settings → Processor rack (17 live processors) lets you build your own chain: add processors with Add processors, reorder them, and switch each on or off. You can also paste parametric-EQ text (for example a headphone correction) to turn it into rack filters.

Upscaler

Settings → Upscaler restores some of the high frequencies that lossy files (MP3, AAC) lose. It finds where the file was cut and rebuilds the missing top octave. Choose how much comes back, or Off to leave the signal untouched.

If the sliders don't change anything: check that the Tone card is on My sound, and that Raw output (Bit-perfect settings) is off — Raw output skips all processing on purpose.

8. Lyrics

Hifinis shows lyrics stored in the song file or in a matching .lrc file next to it, line by line in time with the music.

  • See them: tap the lyrics button on Now Playing. Synced lyrics highlight the current line; tap a line to jump there.
  • Floating lyrics: Settings → Lock screen & widgets → Lyrics overlay — Off, In the app (a small pill on every screen), or Over other apps (needs Android's Display over other apps permission).
  • Out of sync? On the lyrics screen, use Sync offset · this song to move all lines earlier or later. It is saved for that song only.

Adding lyrics to a song

Open the lyrics screen's menu (or ⋮ → Edit tags → Lyrics):

OptionUse it when
Find lyrics onlineYou've set a lyrics server; pick the right result and it is saved into the file
Import from fileYou have an .lrc, .srt, .ttml or .txt file for the song
Paste lyricsYou copied lyrics from somewhere
Search the webYou want to find them in a browser first
Edit lyrics / Edit & sync linesYou want to type them, or time each line while the song plays
Remove imported lyricsYou want to go back to the file's own lyrics

Lyrics server: set it once under Settings → Lock screen & widgets → Lyrics server (any LRCLIB-compatible address). Hifinis doesn't fetch lyrics from the internet unless you set one.

Timing lines yourself

In Edit & sync lines, play the song and tap each line as it is sung. You can also give a line an End time, so the highlight stops when the singing stops. Hifinis saves it as a standard LRC file other players can read.

  2. Fill in the fields (address, user name, password). Display name is just what Hifinis calls it.
  3. Open the source to browse it like your library, and tap a song or station to play.

Notes

  • Passwords are stored on your phone only.
  • In USB Exclusive, a server or cloud song is downloaded to the phone's cache first and then played bit-perfect, so it can take a moment to start. The next song is fetched while the current one plays.
  • Live radio works on every engine, including USB Exclusive, and shows the song title when the station sends it. A station that drops is reconnected by itself.
  • Live radio on Home: a card with Tamil, Tamil · HD, Most listened and more. It is off by default — turn it on under Settings → Look and feel → Live radio on Home.

Casting to a TV or speaker

Tap the Cast button at the top of Now Playing to send the music to a Chromecast, a Google/Nest speaker, or a DLNA TV or AV receiver on the same Wi-Fi. Playback, pause and skip stay on your phone; the sound comes from the other device.

11. Audio Studio

Audio Studio edits, cleans up and masters a song, or builds a multitrack project from recordings and files. Your original file is never changed: every export is a new file.

Open it from Settings → Studio & music world → Audio Studio, or from a song's ⋮ → Audio Studio.

Working on one song

  1. Analysis: Studio first measures loudness, peaks, noise and spectrum. Health check lists what it found, with one-tap fixes (No faults found means a clean master).
  2. Remaster presets set up a whole processing chain at once; Save preset keeps your own.
  3. Trim: drag the white handles on the timeline to set the start and end.
  4. Shape: EQ (drag a band, tap to set, double-tap to zero), stereo width, loudness and the other tools. A section that says Off is not applied until you change one of its controls.
  5. Export a copy: choose the file name, title tag, Format (FLAC, WAV, MP3) and Destination (internal Music, an SD card, a USB drive, or a folder you add). Keep the screen open until it finishes.

Audio cutter

⋮ → Audio cutter on any song: zoom into the waveform, mark the part you want and save it. Where the format allows, the cut is lossless — the kept part is copied without re-encoding.

Multitrack projects

Tap New project on the Studio home. A project has tracks of clips on a timeline:

ButtonWhat it does
Song / FileAdds a song from your library or any audio file as a clip
RecordRecords from the phone's microphone or a USB interface (choose input, sample rate, resolution, channels first)
TakesKeeps several recordings of the same part; pick the best
VersionsSaves the whole project at a point you can go back to
TrackAdds an empty track
MarkAdds a marker at the playhead
  • Editing clips: tap a clip to select it, drag to move it, drag its edges to trim. Split, Cut, Copy, Paste and Duplicate are under the selected clip.
  • Mixing: Mixer (level, pan, mute, solo), Track FX (effects per track), Master (the final bus) and Spectral (see and edit the frequencies).
  • Play renders 30 seconds from the playhead with every effect applied, then plays it.
  • Export mixes the whole project to FLAC, WAV or MP3.

While recording, CLIPPED — lower the input means the input is too loud; lower it and record again.

12. Composer

The Composer writes music with built-in instruments — pianos, strings, winds, brass, Indian instruments, choirs, drums and synths — all played by Hifinis' own sound engine, with no samples to download.

Open it from Settings → Studio & music world → Composer.

Starting a score

  • Start from a template: Empty, Film score, Indian classical, Pop song, Lo-fi beat or South Indian film song. Every track and note stays editable.
  • Your scores lists what you've made. Its ⋮ menu renames, duplicates, deletes or exports a score as a package to share.

The score screen, top to bottom

  1. Header: back, MIDI settings, undo / redo, Export.
  2. Transport: bar.beat position, tempo, time signature and key (tap to change), then back to start, record, play, loop and metronome.
  3. Arrangement: one row per instrument with M (mute) and S (solo); + adds a track. Tap a row to edit that track.
  4. Tabs: Notes, Score, Mixer and Song.
  5. Keyboard: play the selected track live; the arrows change octave. The other buttons open the hands-on instruments.

Writing notes (Notes tab)

ToDo this
Add a noteTap an empty spot in the piano roll
Hear / select a noteTap it
Move itDrag it
Make it longer or shorterDrag its right end (you can grab just past the end; short notes resize from their right half)
Delete itLong-press it

RecordPress record and play the keyboard; notes land on the selected track
  • Grid: 1/1 to 1/32 and triplets (1/8T). Zoom out / in, Quantize, Humanize, +1 / −1 (transpose).
  • Play style for new or selected notes: Normal, Legato, Staccato, Accent, Tremolo, Slide and more.
  • Natural playing (on by default): phrasing, legato joins, a few milliseconds of human timing, and vibrato that grows on long notes.
  • Lanes under the roll: Velocity, Dynamics, Vibrato, Volume, Pan and Reverb — draw them with a finger.
  • Co-Producer writes a bass line, pads, an arpeggio, a counter-melody or drums from your chords. Hum notes turns your humming into notes.

Hands-on instruments

The keyboard buttons open playable screens: Harmonium (pump the bellows), Violin (bow with a finger), Tabla, Winds (blow into the mic), Sitar / Veena, Yaazh and harps, Mridangam, Guitar (frets and strumming), Piano, Drum kit, Bells & bars, and Choir & voice. What you play can be recorded into the score.

Mixer tab

Each track has Level, Pan and Reverb, plus buttons to rename (pencil), move up, duplicate and delete. Tap the instrument name to change it. Two sections open below each track:

  • Synth designer (not on drum tracks): design the track's own sound. Start from a preset — Init saw, Soft pad, Warm keys, Pluck, Fat bass, Soft lead, Wobble, Air — then shape two oscillators (saw, square, triangle, sine, noise), mix, octave, detune, unison and spread, noise, the filter (low-, band- or high-pass, cutoff, resonance, envelope amount, key tracking), the filter and amplitude envelopes, an LFO on the filter, vibrato, glide, mono, drive, width and level. Play the keyboard below to hear each change. Use the instrument again removes the design.
  • EQ & dynamics: a 4-band EQ (low shelf, two bells, high shelf — every band's frequency movable) and a compressor (threshold, ratio, attack, release, make-up). For example, cut 800–2500 Hz on a nasal lead, or use a long attack and short release to soften a pad.

Master at the bottom sets the overall level and the hall size of the shared reverb.

Score and Song tabs

  • Score: the music as sheet notation. Tap the staff to write notes; export a PDF full score, PDF parts for each player, or MusicXML for MuseScore and other notation apps.
  • Song: tempo, time signature, key and named sections (intro, verse, chorus…).

MIDI

Connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard; the MIDI button in the header shows it. Notes, sustain pedal, pitch bend and mod wheel are recorded. MIDI-learn maps your controller's knobs to track volume, pan, reverb, tempo, transport and more. A track can also send its notes out to an external synth.

Exporting

Export offers: FLAC, WAV or MP3 of the whole score; Stems (one WAV per instrument, all the same length, for a DAW); a MIDI file; or Open in Audio Studio to add vocals and master it.

Tip for slower phones: mute the tracks you aren't working on — muted tracks use no processing power, so playback stays smooth.

Running the engine self-test

  1. Play nothing, and choose the output and engine you want to test.
  2. Open Engine self-test and tap Run self-test (about a minute; quiet test noise plays).
  3. Read the results: PASS all good, INFO a note, WARN usually fine, FAIL something went wrong.
  4. Tap Share report to send it to us. The engine log comes with it.

On the phone speaker, wired headphones and Bluetooth, the test proves what Hifinis hands to Android; if Android converts the sound afterwards, the report says so.

14. Extras

Your listening

Settings → Studio & music world → Your listening shows your stats — top songs, artists and albums, and time listened — and Wrapped, a year-in-music story you can preview and share. Scrobbling sends what you play to ListenBrainz (Last.fm support is coming).

Music alarm

Music alarm wakes you with a song or playlist that fades in gently.

Car mode

Settings → Headset, Bluetooth & car → Car mode: a full-screen player with oversized buttons that keeps the screen awake. Turn on Enter on Bluetooth connect to open it automatically in the car, and Exit on disconnect to leave it when you get out.

Buttons and gestures

Settings → Shortcuts & gestures:

  • Swipe gestures on the cover: left / right changes the song, down opens the queue, up opens lyrics.
  • Double tap on the cover and Long press action in lists: choose what they do.
  • Shake to change song.
  • Custom earphone button: for one-button wired earphones, choose what 1, 2, 3 or 4 presses and press-and-hold do.

Lock screen, notification and widgets

Settings → Lock screen & widgets and Notifications:

  • Lock screen player, its style, and album art.
  • Notification style → Volume puts − / + on the lock screen, which is handy for DACs without volume buttons. Detailed adds ±10 s seek buttons.
  • Home-screen widget: add it from your launcher's widget list; Widget style and Widget theme change its look.
  • Synced lyrics can show on the widget, the lock screen, the notification and the mini player.

Moving to a new phone, and backups

  • Settings → Backup & restore → Back up now saves settings, playlists and favourites to a file; Restore backup brings them back. Reset all settings returns to defaults and keeps your music folders.
  • Export Hifinis settings & effects (Headset, Bluetooth & car page) makes one file with settings, EQ and sound profiles, effects, the Processor Rack, every device's settings, playlists and favourites. Import settings & effects restores it on this phone or a new one.
  • Export current sound / Import a sound: share just what's applied right now (EQ, effects, rack, upscaler) as one small file.

15. Troubleshooting and reporting a problem

Most problems have a quick fix below. If yours doesn't, run the engine self-test and share the report with us — it tells us exactly what happened.

ProblemTry this
No sound from the USB DACCheck USB DAC is selected on Home and that Hifinis was allowed to use the device. Tap Reconnect DAC. Close other music apps that may hold the DAC. Still nothing? Switch to Hi-Res
DAC's own buttons or screen stop respondingTap Release DAC, unplug, plug back in. Use Hi-Res with that DAC and send us a log
Sudden loud sound on a DACSet Volume type to Hardware and turn on Ear safety. Keep the app updated — DACs that need it are now fully re-opened on a rate change
Volume keys don't change the DAC levelCheck the DAC's Volume type. With Fixed at 100%, use the DAC's own buttons
Other apps or the ringtone are silentIn USB Exclusive, Hifinis holds the DAC. Tap Release DAC, or turn on Release while paused
A short burst of sound when skipping while pausedFixed in 2.5.11 — update the app
✓ HIFINIS PURE badge missingSomething changes the sound: EQ, Tone, effects, software volume below 100%, or a rate the DAC can't play. Turn them off, or use hardware volume
EQ or effects do nothingSet the Tone card to My sound, and turn off Raw output
Constant noise from a USB headset in ExclusiveUpdate to 2.5.11. If it continues, use Hi-Res and send us a self-test report
Songs are missing from the librarySettings → Library → Music folders: add their folder. Then Re-read tags from your music folders
Lyrics don't show or are out of timeAdd them (Lyrics section), then use Sync offset · this song
Composer playback stuttersMute the tracks you aren't working on; turn on Performance mode
Music stops when you open another appSettings → Audio & output → Audio focus: turn on Resume if the other app stays quiet

Reporting a problem

  1. Reproduce the problem once, if you can.
  2. Open Workshop → Engine self-test, run it, and tap Share report. The report and the engine log are included in the shared message and as an attached file — please send the file too.
  3. Tell us your phone, your DAC or headphones, which engine you used (Android, Hi-Res, USB Exclusive), and what you heard.
  4. Send it in the Curvio Community Telegram group, or through Hifinis' Google Play page.

Send the report before restarting the phone or the DAC — a restart clears the log we need.
