Hifinis

Hi-Res Music Player for Android · by Curvio Studio

An offline music player for people who care how their music sounds. It plays your files the way they were recorded and gives you real control over the signal path — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Download for Android Telegram updates

Free beta · Android 8 or newer · pick Hifinis-…-arm64.apk (almost every phone). The app tells you when a new version is out and updates itself. Google Play release coming soon.

Bit-perfect playback

  • Three output engines: Android, Native (Oboe, low-latency) and Exclusive USB
  • Exclusive USB streams straight to your USB DAC, bypassing Android's mixer and its forced resampling
  • Plays at the file's own sample rate where the DAC supports it — up to 384 kHz / 32-bit, plus native DSD
  • Separate DAC volume with a gentle fade-in

Formats

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, APE, DSF/DFF, MP3, AAC/M4A, Ogg, Opus, WMA, MKA.

Sound shaping

  • Parametric EQ with drag-the-curve editing and a live spectrum
  • Graphic EQ (10, 15 or 31 bands) with 100+ searchable presets
  • Hifinis Enhance — 100 voicings: warmth, air, bass shelf, vocal lift and more
  • Per-output sound profiles, crossfade, gapless, ReplayGain, A-B repeat

Library

  • Folder-based — Hifinis only sees the folders you choose
  • Albums, artists, genres, playlists, favourites and a built-in tag editor
  • Reads .m3u, .m3u8, .pls, .xspf and .wpl playlist files
  • Synced lyrics with on-device translation
  • Optional home servers and network shares: Jellyfin, Plex, Subsonic, SMB, WebDAV, DLNA

Privacy

No analytics, no ad SDKs, no user accounts. Your library, play history and settings never leave your device. Read the full privacy policy.
