Everything Hifinis does

Built for people who care how their music sounds — and simple enough for everyone else.

🎧Bit-perfect sound

  • Three engines: Android, Bit-perfect (AAudio) and Exclusive USB
  • Exclusive USB talks straight to your DAC — past Android's mixer and its forced resampling
  • Every song at its own sample rate, up to 384 kHz / 32-bit — the DAC switches rate song by song
  • Picks the clean integer multiple when the DAC lacks the exact rate (44.1 → 88.2 kHz, not 48)
  • DSD as DoP or native DSD on DACs that support it
  • Android 14+ bit-perfect mixer path, so even the Android engine can stay untouched

🔬See it, prove it

  • Signal path: every stage from file to ears, live — marked clean or changing the sound
  • Engine self-test that plays test tones and proves the output is bit-exact
  • Live DAC report: class, real sample rates, bit depth, DSD support
  • Signal Lab and spectrogram (Spek-style) to check any file
  • Library Lab finds fake hi-res files, duplicates (even by sound) and loudness

🔌Made for DACs & IEMs

  • Separate DAC volume, the DAC's own hardware volume, and an ear-safety start level
  • Volume curves for sensitive in-ears (IEM −80 dB, Studio −90 dB) and 0.5 % steps
  • Every DAC, Bluetooth headphone and wired headset keeps its own EQ, effects and volume
  • USB-C earphones with a mic and noise cancelling keep working
  • Pauses when you unplug — never jumps to the speaker — and resumes where you left off
  • Reconnect / release the DAC in one tap

📡Every output at once

  • Play on your DAC, Bluetooth speaker and phone speaker at the same time
  • Each output with its own level, channel and sync delay
  • Cast to Chromecast, Google TV and Nest speakers
  • Pauses for other apps — or share outputs with them, your choice

🎚️Equalizer like no other

  • Graphic EQ in 5, 10, 15 or 31 bands, ±15 dB
  • Parametric EQ with up to 31 filters — peak, shelves, low/high pass, notch — drag the curve
  • 120 presets incl. Harman Target and 20 Hifinis originals, searchable by name or by sound
  • Live spectrum while you tune, pre-amp with auto headroom
  • Works on Bit-perfect and USB output, not just the phone speaker

✨Effects & voicing

  • Hifinis Enhance — 100 voicings: warmth, air, bass shelf, vocal lift and more
  • Processor Rack: chain stereo width, reverb, limiter and headphone correction
  • Upscaler that gently restores the top end of lossy files
  • Crossfeed, spatial widening, loudness, reverb and L/R balance
  • Volume boost for quiet Bluetooth speakers, with a safe limiter

🎤Lyrics you can read

Questions

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer on 64-bit phones (almost every phone from the last several years). Install it from Google Play and it picks the right version for your phone.

Does it work with my USB DAC?

Hifinis works with standard USB Audio Class DACs and dongles. Exclusive USB mode plays bit-perfect at the file's own rate where the DAC supports it, up to 384 kHz / 32-bit.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Hifinis reads only the folders you choose and never sends your library anywhere. Optional online lyrics, artist images and the update check are explained in the privacy policy.

How do updates work?

Through Google Play, like any other app. Installed an APK from this site before? Install from Google Play over it — your library, playlists, EQ and settings are kept.
